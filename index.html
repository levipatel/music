Music Composer | Producer | Arranger | Songwriter

Hello, my name is Levi Patel. I love to create beautiful music here in New Zealand, typically combining intimate guitar and piano composition with richly harmonic strings and delicate textures.

Besides my own music, I produce and engineer for other artists, arrange strings, co-write songs, and compose for film and television. I'm also building a robot.

Thank you for visiting, and I hope you'll enjoy what you find.

Gravity (Music Video)

After over a decade of releasing instrumental music, I've finally found my voice. This is Gravity, the first single from my upcoming third album.

Through Winter Eyes (Music Video)

Creating my own music is how I explore my emotions and the constantly circling sounds inside my head.

Through Winter Eyes is an intimate piano and cello duet from my album A Shifting Lightness, featuring a breathtaking cello performance and accompanied by this beautiful video by Jonathan Zsofi, Yonoko, Josh Allen, and their team.

Alayna - Cherry Tree (Songwriting, Production, Arrangement)

When I think of my most rewarding musical experiences, they often come from working with other people.

I wrote and produced Cherry Tree with Alayna and her long time collaborator Noema Te Hau III, and the day we wrote this song was one of the most special in my life. We then recorded the instrumentation at my studio, complete with a stunning string trio, and later filmed a live studio performance.
