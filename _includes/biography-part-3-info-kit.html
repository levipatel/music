Now, the voice behind Patel's modest spectacles and quiet demeanour will be shared with the world through his third album, Sailing on the Light of a Passing Star.

It's an evolution that Patel himself could barely have imagined three years ago, but after undergoing an intensive regime of vocal development and endless lyrical iterations, his first singer-songwriter album has arrived as an intimate, ethereal travelogue through the journey from heartbreak to hope.
